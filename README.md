Purpose of This Section 
The Insights section exists to do two things: help current and prospective clients make better 
decisions about their technology and branding, and establish Zebnex as a credible, 
knowledgeable voice in the space. Posts should be genuinely useful — practical tips, clear 
explanations, honest takes — rather than thinly-veiled sales pitches. This is also a strong, low
cost SEO asset: well-written posts targeting real search terms ("how to secure a small business 
network," "when to rebrand," "cloud vs on-premise for SMEs") bring in organic traffic long after 
they're published. 
Content Categories 
Tech Trends Coverage of emerging technology relevant to business owners — AI adoption, 
automation, new tools worth knowing about — explained without unnecessary jargon.

awareness, what a small business actually needs (vs. what's overkill), how to respond if 
something goes wrong.

and what makes certain brands memorable while others fade into the background.

infrastructure — costs, benefits, common pitfalls, and how to plan a migration properly.
